## Standard cleaning maintains

A standard cleaning is best when a home is already in good condition and needs consistent upkeep.

## Deep cleaning resets

A deep clean adds detail work such as baseboards, cabinet fronts, heavier bathroom buildup, and kitchen degreasing.

## First-time clients often benefit from deep cleaning

Starting with a deep clean creates a strong baseline for future recurring visits.
