## Start with the rooms you use most

Focus on the kitchen, bathrooms, and main living areas first. These spaces create the biggest sense of calm when they are reset.

## Work in layers

Remove clutter, dust high surfaces, wipe touch points, clean floors, and finish with small details like folded throws and refreshed counters.

## Keep a maintenance rhythm

A recurring cleaning plan helps your spring reset last longer and keeps deep cleaning from becoming overwhelming.
