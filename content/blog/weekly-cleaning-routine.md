## Choose one reset day

Pick a day that fits your household rhythm. Consistency matters more than perfection.

## Prioritize surfaces and floors

Clear counters, sanitize sinks, wipe bathroom fixtures, vacuum traffic paths, and mop hard floors.

## Add a monthly detail

Baseboards, appliance fronts, interior windows, and cabinet fronts can rotate monthly.
